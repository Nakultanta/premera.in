# PREMERA — preliminary public-web name search (12 October 2026)

## Result
**Status: not cleared; research is preliminary.** A general public-web search did not establish a specific Indian ladies-suit business operating under the exact word PREMERA. Public search results are not complete trademark clearance, and absence of an indexed result proves nothing.

## Potentially relevant findings
- Publicly accessible US trademark databases list **PREMERA** as a live/registered mark for healthcare / insurance services (US Registration 2,132,965; International Class 36). Source: https://trademarks.justia.com/746/33/premera-74633898.html
- A separate US record lists **PREMERA** for coatings used to minimize corrosion and abrasion (US Registration 5,096,868; International Class 2). Source: https://trademarks.justia.com/867/81/premera-86781717.html
- These records are in different goods/services categories and jurisdictions. They do not by themselves establish whether PREMERA can be used or registered for ladies' suits in India; they do demonstrate that the term is already in use by third parties.
