# PREMERA — Brand Guidelines (Stage 2 draft)

## Current working identity
**Primary wordmark:** PREMERA (plain Latin letters; no accent). Supporting text: **LADIES SUITS**. Tagline: **Grace in Every Drape**. Signature symbol: sculpted P monogram integrated with a peacock feather.

The updated image is a revised concept that reflects the new name. Confirm the exact wordmark and legal name availability before authorising bulk print production.

## Brand personality
Luxury, feminine, poised, traditional craftsmanship, contemporary drape. The peacock feather is the signature secondary motif. Metallic gold and ivory establish the luxury tone; teal and blue provide distinctive accents.

### Digital metallic gold recipe
For a digital gold gradient use `#5C2D05` → `#A1631A` → `#F6D68C` → `#C3832D` → `#5C2D05`. HEX cannot create real reflectivity. For physical printing, request a foil or metallic-ink proof rather than relying on standard CMYK alone.

## Typography proposal (not source-font identification)
- **Display / wordmark:** Bodoni Moda SemiBold 600. Optical letter spacing may need adjustment to match the logo.
- **Descriptor / digital body:** Montserrat Medium 500. For “LADIES SUITS”, use uppercase and about 0.20–0.28em letter spacing.
- **Tagline / editorial accent:** Cormorant Garamond Medium Italic 500.
- The image’s original lettering is not reliably identifiable from a flattened raster; these are free Google Fonts substitutes.

## Logo variants
1. **Primary concept:** full PREMERA logo on Warm Ivory.
2. **Icon crop:** P monogram plus peacock feather for social avatars, after checking small-size legibility.
3. **Wordmark crop:** PREMERA alone for simple placements.
4. **Descriptor and tagline:** use when space and contrast allow.
5. **Simplified vector feather:** independent secondary motif, not a replacement for the detailed source symbol.

## Clear space and minimum size (working guidance)
Reserve clear space around the full mark at least equal to the height of the “E” in PREMERA. Keep text, seams, folds, embroidery, and graphics outside that area. Avoid the full detailed raster for tiny embroidery; as a starting point, test at 35 mm wide or larger and perform a stitch test. On digital placements, test the full mark around 240 px wide. These are starting guidelines and should be validated by the printer or embroiderer.

## Do / don't
**Do:** preserve proportions, colour relationships, and typography spacing; use ivory/light surfaces or commission a properly prepared dark-background variant; use teal/blue feather colours as accents.

**Don't:** stretch the logo, recolour individual pieces arbitrarily, apply a second fake gold effect, or place the detailed mark on a busy photograph.

## Production status
The master is a 1254 × 1254 flattened raster. Transparent cuts are approximate, intended for mockups and digital compositing, and may not retain every pale highlight. Raster-backed SVG wrappers are not path-based vectors. The editable text-lockup SVG is a reconstruction, not the approved image rendered into perfect paths. Commission manual vector reconstruction before signage, foil dies, laser cutting, or embroidery.
